June 2025 grade boundaries at a glance

The June 2025 series has boundaries for 50 main papers plus 8 time-zone variants across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting, Information Technology and Psychology (Law is graded on both papers combined). A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with January 2025, the A boundary went up in 28 papers, down in 11 and stayed the same in 7 of the 46 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11), up 19 marks from 134 to 153 out of 200; the biggest fall was P4 Pure Mathematics 4 (WMA14), down 5 marks from 61 to 56 out of 75.
