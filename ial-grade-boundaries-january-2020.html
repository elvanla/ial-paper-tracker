January 2020 grade boundaries at a glance

The January 2020 series has boundaries for 33 main papers across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting and Psychology. A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with October 2019, the A boundary went up in 7 papers, down in 10 and stayed the same in 6 of the 23 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11), up 10 marks from 124 to 134 out of 200; the biggest fall was U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), down 24 marks from 152 to 128 out of 200.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 64/75 (85%) on S2 Statistics 2 (WST02), lowest 52/75 (69%) on P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12); highest A* boundary 69/75 on S2 Statistics 2 (WST02); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WME01 A boundary up 5 marks since October 2019 (55 → 60); lowest raw mark for full UMS 64/75 on WMA12.
  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 65/75 (87%) on F1 Further Pure Mathematics 1 (WFM01), lowest 64/75 (85%) on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); highest A* boundary 69/75 on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM01 A boundary up 4 marks since June 2019 (61 → 65).
  • Physics: highest A boundary 55/80 (69%) on U2 Waves and Electricity (WPH12), lowest 48/80 (60%) on U1 Mechanics and Materials (WPH11); highest A* boundary 61/90 on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPH12 A boundary up 5 marks since October 2019 (50 → 55); lowest raw mark for full UMS 56/80 on WPH11.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 67/90 (74%) on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14), lowest 55/80 (69%) on U2 Energetics, Group Chemistry, Halogenoalkanes and Alcohols (WCH12); highest A* boundary 73/90 on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH11 A boundary down 5 marks since October 2019 (64 → 59); lowest raw mark for full UMS 67/80 on WCH12.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 37/50 (74%) on U3 Practical Skills in Biology I (WBI13), lowest 47/80 (59%) on U2 Cells, Development, Biodiversity and Conservation (WBI12); highest A* boundary 59/90 on U4 Energy, Environment, Microbiology and Immunity (WBI14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI12 A boundary down 5 marks since October 2019 (52 → 47); lowest raw mark for full UMS 55/80 on WBI12.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 58/80 (72%) on U1 Markets in Action (WEC11), lowest 50/80 (62%) on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); highest A* boundary 55/80 on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC11 A boundary down 1 mark since October 2019 (59 → 58); lowest raw mark for full UMS 60/80 on WEC13.
  • Business: highest A boundary 54/80 (68%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11), lowest 49/80 (61%) on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13); highest A* boundary 54/80 on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS11 A boundary down 2 marks since October 2019 (56 → 54); lowest raw mark for full UMS 59/80 on WBS13.
  • Accounting: highest A boundary 134/200 (67%) on U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11), lowest 128/200 (64%) on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12); highest A* boundary 145/200 on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WAC12 A boundary down 24 marks since October 2019 (152 → 128); lowest raw mark for full UMS 162/200 on WAC12.
  • Psychology: highest A boundary 35/64 (55%) on U3 Applications of Psychology (WPS03), lowest 44/96 (46%) on U4 Clinical Psychology and Psychological Skills (WPS04); highest A* boundary 39/64 on U3 Applications of Psychology (WPS03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPS01 A boundary up 3 marks since October 2019 (30 → 33); lowest raw mark for full UMS 56/96 on WPS04.
