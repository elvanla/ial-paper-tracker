Privacy notice

IAL Paper Tracker is a free, non-commercial study tool. This page explains what it stores about you and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your email address, and either a password or your Google sign-in, so you can sign in. Passwords are stored as a secure hash by our login provider; nobody can read them. If you use Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and we only use the email.
  • What you choose to enter: your subjects, the papers you've logged (marks, dates, notes), any papers or subjects you add yourself, and your exam dates.

We don't ask for your school, location or anything else, and we don't use ads or tracking cookies.

Who can see it

Only you. The database only lets a signed-in account read and change its own records. We never sell or share your data.

Your data is held by Supabase (login and database) and the website is served by Vercel. They process it only to run the service.

Deleting your data

Open the account menu (top right) and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything you've logged, straight away.

Under 13?

Please get a parent or guardian's permission before creating an account.

Grade boundaries

Grades are worked out from Pearson Edexcel's published IAL grade boundaries. This site isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson. Always check important results against the official documents.
