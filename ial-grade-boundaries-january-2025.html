January 2025 grade boundaries at a glance

The January 2025 series has boundaries for 46 main papers across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting and Psychology. A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with October 2024, the A boundary went up in 12 papers, down in 11 and stayed the same in 13 of the 36 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was P4 Pure Mathematics 4 (WMA14), up 9 marks from 52 to 61 out of 75; the biggest fall was U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11), down 5 marks from 139 to 134 out of 200.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 64/75 (85%) on M1 Mechanics 1 (WME01), lowest 54/75 (72%) on P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12); highest A* boundary 68/75 on P4 Pure Mathematics 4 (WMA14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WMA14 A boundary up 9 marks since October 2024 (52 → 61); lowest raw mark for full UMS 68/75 on WMA12.
  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 66/75 (88%) on S3 Statistics 3 (WST03), lowest 62/75 (83%) on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); highest A* boundary 70/75 on S3 Statistics 3 (WST03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM03 A boundary up 7 marks since June 2024 (55 → 62).
  • Physics: highest A boundary 71/90 (79%) on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14), lowest 59/80 (74%) on U2 Waves and Electricity (WPH12); highest A* boundary 79/90 on U5 Thermodynamics, Radiation, Oscillations and Cosmology (WPH15); every main paper kept the same A boundary as its previous sitting; lowest raw mark for full UMS 75/80 on WPH11.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 66/90 (73%) on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14), lowest 30/50 (60%) on U6 Practical Skills in Chemistry II (WCH16); highest A* boundary 73/90 on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH15 A boundary up 8 marks since October 2024 (57 → 65); lowest raw mark for full UMS 40/50 on WCH16.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 38/50 (76%) on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16), lowest 53/90 (59%) on U4 Energy, Environment, Microbiology and Immunity (WBI14); highest A* boundary 42/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI11 A boundary up 3 marks since October 2024 (46 → 49); lowest raw mark for full UMS 65/90 on WBI14.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 55/80 (69%) on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13), lowest 51/80 (64%) on U2 Macroeconomic Performance and Policy (WEC12); highest A* boundary 60/80 on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC12 A boundary down 4 marks since October 2024 (55 → 51); lowest raw mark for full UMS 61/80 on WEC12.
  • Business: highest A boundary 54/80 (68%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11), lowest 42/80 (52%) on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13); highest A* boundary 57/80 on U4 Global Business (WBS14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS12 A boundary down 3 marks since October 2024 (54 → 51); lowest raw mark for full UMS 50/80 on WBS13.
  • Accounting: highest A boundary 147/200 (74%) on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), lowest 134/200 (67%) on U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11); highest A* boundary 164/200 on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WAC11 A boundary down 5 marks since October 2024 (139 → 134); lowest raw mark for full UMS 180/200 on WAC11.
  • Psychology: highest A boundary 32/64 (50%) on U1 Social and Cognitive Psychology (WPS01), lowest 39/96 (41%) on U4 Clinical Psychology and Psychological Skills (WPS04); highest A* boundary 35/64 on U3 Applications of Psychology (WPS03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPS04 A boundary down 7 marks since June 2024 (46 → 39); lowest raw mark for full UMS 53/96 on WPS04.
