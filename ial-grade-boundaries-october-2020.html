October 2020 grade boundaries at a glance

The October 2020 series has boundaries for 49 main papers across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting, Information Technology and Psychology (Law is graded on both papers combined). A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with January 2020, the A boundary went up in 2 papers, down in 30 and stayed the same in 0 of the 32 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13), up 7 marks from 49 to 56 out of 80; the biggest fall was U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), down 30 marks from 128 to 98 out of 200.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 55/75 (73%) on P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12), lowest 42/75 (56%) on S1 Statistics 1 (WST01); highest A* boundary 64/75 on P3 Pure Mathematics 3 (WMA13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WST02 A boundary down 15 marks since January 2020 (64 → 49); lowest raw mark for full UMS 60/75 on WST01.
  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 55/75 (73%) on S3 Statistics 3 (WST03), lowest 52/75 (69%) on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); highest A* boundary 64/75 on S3 Statistics 3 (WST03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM02 A boundary down 16 marks since June 2019 (68 → 52); lowest raw mark for full UMS 70/75 on WME03.
  • Physics: highest A boundary 54/90 (60%) on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14), lowest 38/80 (48%) on U1 Mechanics and Materials (WPH11); highest A* boundary 64/90 on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPH12 A boundary down 12 marks since January 2020 (55 → 43); lowest raw mark for full UMS 46/80 on WPH11.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 61/90 (68%) on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14), lowest 44/80 (55%) on U2 Energetics, Group Chemistry, Halogenoalkanes and Alcohols (WCH12); highest A* boundary 73/90 on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH12 A boundary down 11 marks since January 2020 (55 → 44); lowest raw mark for full UMS 58/80 on WCH12.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 39/50 (78%) on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16), lowest 46/90 (51%) on U5 Respiration, Internal Environment, Coordination and Gene Technology (WBI15); highest A* boundary 44/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI11 A boundary down 7 marks since January 2020 (48 → 41); lowest raw mark for full UMS 55/80 on WBI11.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 54/80 (68%) on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14), lowest 46/80 (58%) on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); highest A* boundary 62/80 on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC11 A boundary down 6 marks since January 2020 (58 → 52); lowest raw mark for full UMS 64/80 on WEC13.
  • Business: highest A boundary 56/80 (70%) on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13), lowest 47/80 (59%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11); highest A* boundary 62/80 on U4 Global Business (WBS14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS13 A boundary up 7 marks since January 2020 (49 → 56); lowest raw mark for full UMS 65/80 on WBS11.
