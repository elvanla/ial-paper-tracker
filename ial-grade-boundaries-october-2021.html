October 2021 grade boundaries at a glance

The October 2021 series has boundaries for 46 main papers across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting and Information Technology. A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with January 2021, the A boundary went up in 5 papers, down in 25 and stayed the same in 12 of the 42 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was S2 Statistics 2 (WST02), up 2 marks from 47 to 49 out of 75; the biggest fall was F2 Further Pure Mathematics 2 (WFM02), down 10 marks from 52 to 42 out of 75.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 53/75 (71%) on P4 Pure Mathematics 4 (WMA14), lowest 37/75 (49%) on S1 Statistics 1 (WST01); highest A* boundary 64/75 on P4 Pure Mathematics 4 (WMA14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WST01 A boundary down 7 marks since January 2021 (44 → 37); lowest raw mark for full UMS 53/75 on WST01.
  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 53/75 (71%) on S3 Statistics 3 (WST03), lowest 42/75 (56%) on F2 Further Pure Mathematics 2 (WFM02); highest A* boundary 64/75 on F3 Further Pure Mathematics 3 (WFM03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM02 A boundary down 10 marks since January 2021 (52 → 42); lowest raw mark for full UMS 64/75 on WFM01.
  • Physics: highest A boundary 55/90 (61%) on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14), lowest 38/80 (48%) on U1 Mechanics and Materials (WPH11); highest A* boundary 66/90 on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPH11 A boundary up 1 mark since January 2021 (37 → 38); lowest raw mark for full UMS 46/80 on WPH11.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 34/50 (68%) on U6 Practical Skills in Chemistry II (WCH16), lowest 48/90 (53%) on U5 Transition Metals and Organic Nitrogen Chemistry (WCH15); highest A* boundary 41/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Chemistry II (WCH16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH11 A boundary down 4 marks since January 2021 (55 → 51); lowest raw mark for full UMS 35/50 on WCH13.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 39/50 (78%) on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16), lowest 41/90 (46%) on U5 Respiration, Internal Environment, Coordination and Gene Technology (WBI15); highest A* boundary 42/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI13 A boundary down 6 marks since January 2021 (33 → 27); lowest raw mark for full UMS 49/80 on WBI11.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 52/80 (65%) on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14), lowest 44/80 (55%) on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); highest A* boundary 61/80 on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC13 A boundary down 3 marks since January 2021 (47 → 44); lowest raw mark for full UMS 62/80 on WEC13.
  • Business: highest A boundary 56/80 (70%) on U4 Global Business (WBS14), lowest 43/80 (54%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11); highest A* boundary 63/80 on U4 Global Business (WBS14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS11 A boundary down 4 marks since January 2021 (47 → 43); lowest raw mark for full UMS 57/80 on WBS11.
