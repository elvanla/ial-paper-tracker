October 2019 grade boundaries at a glance

The October 2019 series has boundaries for 23 main papers across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting and Psychology (Law is graded on both papers combined). A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with June 2019, the A boundary went up in 12 papers, down in 8 and stayed the same in 3 of the 23 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), up 19 marks from 133 to 152 out of 200; the biggest fall was M1 Mechanics 1 (WME01), down 8 marks from 63 to 55 out of 75.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 56/75 (75%) on P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12), lowest 52/75 (69%) on P1 Pure Mathematics 1 (WMA11); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WME01 A boundary down 8 marks since June 2019 (63 → 55); lowest raw mark for full UMS 66/75 on WMA11.
  • Physics: highest A boundary 50/80 (62%) on U2 Waves and Electricity (WPH12), lowest 44/80 (55%) on U1 Mechanics and Materials (WPH11); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPH13 A boundary down 4 marks since June 2019 (32 → 28); lowest raw mark for full UMS 52/80 on WPH11.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 64/80 (80%) on U1 Structure, Bonding and Introduction to Organic Chemistry (WCH11), lowest 37/50 (74%) on U3 Practical Skills in Chemistry I (WCH13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH12 A boundary up 3 marks since June 2019 (57 → 60); lowest raw mark for full UMS 72/80 on WCH12.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 37/50 (74%) on U3 Practical Skills in Biology I (WBI13), lowest 50/80 (62%) on U1 Molecules, Diet, Transport and Health (WBI11); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI11 A boundary up 3 marks since June 2019 (47 → 50); lowest raw mark for full UMS 58/80 on WBI11.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 59/80 (74%) on U1 Markets in Action (WEC11), lowest 57/80 (71%) on U2 Macroeconomic Performance and Policy (WEC12); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC11 A boundary up 2 marks since June 2019 (57 → 59); lowest raw mark for full UMS 69/80 on WEC12.
  • Business: highest A boundary 56/80 (70%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11), lowest 51/80 (64%) on U2 Managing Business Activities (WBS12); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS11 A boundary down 1 mark since June 2019 (57 → 56); lowest raw mark for full UMS 61/80 on WBS12.
  • Accounting: highest A boundary 152/200 (76%) on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), lowest 124/200 (62%) on U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11); highest A* boundary 165/200 on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WAC12 A boundary up 19 marks since June 2019 (133 → 152); lowest raw mark for full UMS 156/200 on WAC11.
  • Psychology: highest A boundary 35/64 (55%) on U3 Applications of Psychology (WPS03), lowest 44/96 (46%) on U4 Clinical Psychology and Psychological Skills (WPS04); highest A* boundary 39/64 on U3 Applications of Psychology (WPS03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPS01 A boundary up 2 marks since June 2019 (28 → 30); lowest raw mark for full UMS 36/64 on WPS01.
