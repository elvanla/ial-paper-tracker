June 2026 grade boundaries at a glance

The June 2026 series has boundaries for 50 main papers plus 38 time-zone variants across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting, Information Technology and Psychology (Law is graded on both papers combined). A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with January 2026, the A boundary went up in 26 papers, down in 15 and stayed the same in 5 of the 46 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), up 23 marks from 140 to 163 out of 200; the biggest fall was U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11), down 10 marks from 137 to 127 out of 200.

  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 66/75 (88%) on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03), lowest 57/75 (76%) on F3 Further Pure Mathematics 3 (WFM03); highest A* boundary 70/75 on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM03 A boundary down 6 marks since January 2026 (63 → 57); lowest raw mark for full UMS 67/75 on WFM03.
