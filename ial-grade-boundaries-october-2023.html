October 2023 grade boundaries at a glance

The October 2023 series has boundaries for 36 main papers across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business and Accounting. A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with June 2023, the A boundary went up in 4 papers, down in 8 and stayed the same in 24 of the 36 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12), up 4 marks from 51 to 55 out of 75; the biggest fall was P3 Pure Mathematics 3 (WMA13), down 2 marks from 61 to 59 out of 75.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 65/75 (87%) on M1 Mechanics 1 (WME01), lowest 52/75 (69%) on P1 Pure Mathematics 1 (WMA11); highest A* boundary 66/75 on P3 Pure Mathematics 3 (WMA13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WMA12 A boundary up 4 marks since June 2023 (51 → 55); lowest raw mark for full UMS 66/75 on WMA11.
  • Physics: highest A boundary 73/90 (81%) on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14), lowest 33/50 (66%) on U3 Practical Skills in Physics I (WPH13); highest A* boundary 81/90 on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14); every main paper kept the same A boundary as its previous sitting; lowest raw mark for full UMS 65/80 on WPH11.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 65/90 (72%) on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14), lowest 51/80 (64%) on U2 Energetics, Group Chemistry, Halogenoalkanes and Alcohols (WCH12); highest A* boundary 72/90 on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH12 A boundary up 1 mark since June 2023 (50 → 51); lowest raw mark for full UMS 63/80 on WCH12.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 43/50 (86%) on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16), lowest 45/90 (50%) on U4 Energy, Environment, Microbiology and Immunity (WBI14); highest A* boundary 46/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI13 A boundary down 2 marks since June 2023 (39 → 37); lowest raw mark for full UMS 49/90 on WBI14.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 54/80 (68%) on U1 Markets in Action (WEC11), lowest 50/80 (62%) on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); highest A* boundary 56/80 on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC14 A boundary down 2 marks since June 2023 (52 → 50); lowest raw mark for full UMS 60/80 on WEC13.
  • Business: highest A boundary 60/80 (75%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11), lowest 43/80 (54%) on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13); highest A* boundary 56/80 on U4 Global Business (WBS14); every main paper kept the same A boundary as its previous sitting; lowest raw mark for full UMS 53/80 on WBS13.
  • Accounting: highest A boundary 149/200 (74%) on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), lowest 117/200 (58%) on U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11); highest A* boundary 168/200 on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12); every main paper kept the same A boundary as its previous sitting; lowest raw mark for full UMS 155/200 on WAC11.
