January 2026 grade boundaries at a glance

The January 2026 series has boundaries for 46 main papers plus 42 time-zone variants across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting and Psychology. A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with October 2025, the A boundary went up in 11 papers, down in 15 and stayed the same in 10 of the 36 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14), up 11 marks from 61 to 72 out of 90; the biggest fall was U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), down 19 marks from 159 to 140 out of 200.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 66/75 (88%) on S1 Statistics 1 (WST01), lowest 51/75 (68%) on P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12); highest A* boundary 67/75 on M2 Mechanics 2 (WME02); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WDM11 A boundary down 5 marks since June 2025 (63 → 58); lowest raw mark for full UMS 63/75 on WMA12.
  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 66/75 (88%) on F2 Further Pure Mathematics 2 (WFM02), lowest 62/75 (83%) on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); highest A* boundary 70/75 on S3 Statistics 3 (WST03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM03 A boundary up 5 marks since June 2025 (58 → 63).
  • Physics: highest A boundary 71/90 (79%) on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14), lowest 36/50 (72%) on U3 Practical Skills in Physics I (WPH13); highest A* boundary 80/90 on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPH13 A boundary down 1 mark since October 2025 (37 → 36); lowest raw mark for full UMS 44/50 on WPH13.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 72/90 (80%) on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14), lowest 35/50 (70%) on U3 Practical Skills in Chemistry I (WCH13); highest A* boundary 81/90 on U4 Rates, Equilibria and Further Organic Chemistry (WCH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH14 A boundary up 11 marks since October 2025 (61 → 72); lowest raw mark for full UMS 80/90 on WCH15.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 38/50 (76%) on U3 Practical Skills in Biology I (WBI13), lowest 55/90 (61%) on U4 Energy, Environment, Microbiology and Immunity (WBI14); highest A* boundary 43/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI12 A boundary up 2 marks since October 2025 (55 → 57); lowest raw mark for full UMS 67/90 on WBI14.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 57/80 (71%) on U1 Markets in Action (WEC11), lowest 52/80 (65%) on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14); highest A* boundary 60/80 on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC11 A boundary up 7 marks since October 2025 (50 → 57); lowest raw mark for full UMS 62/80 on WEC14.
  • Business: highest A boundary 54/80 (68%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11), lowest 45/80 (56%) on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13); highest A* boundary 55/80 on U4 Global Business (WBS14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS14 A boundary down 4 marks since October 2025 (52 → 48); lowest raw mark for full UMS 55/80 on WBS13.
  • Accounting: highest A boundary 140/200 (70%) on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12), lowest 137/200 (68%) on U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11); highest A* boundary 157/200 on U2 Corporate and Management Accounting (WAC12); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WAC12 A boundary down 19 marks since October 2025 (159 → 140); lowest raw mark for full UMS 174/200 on WAC12.
  • Psychology: highest A boundary 32/64 (50%) on U1 Social and Cognitive Psychology (WPS01), lowest 39/96 (41%) on U4 Clinical Psychology and Psychological Skills (WPS04); highest A* boundary 35/64 on U3 Applications of Psychology (WPS03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPS02 A boundary down 6 marks since June 2025 (49 → 43); lowest raw mark for full UMS 53/96 on WPS04.
