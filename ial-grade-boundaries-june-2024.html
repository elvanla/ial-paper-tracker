June 2024 grade boundaries at a glance

The June 2024 series has boundaries for 50 main papers plus 4 time-zone variants across Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Business, Accounting, Information Technology and Psychology (Law is graded on both papers combined). A higher boundary usually means students found the paper more accessible, and a lower one usually means a harder paper.

Compared with January 2024, the A boundary went up in 23 papers, down in 19 and stayed the same in 4 of the 46 main papers sat in both series. The biggest rise was U1 The Accounting System and Costing (WAC11), up 18 marks from 117 to 135 out of 200; the biggest fall was M2 Mechanics 2 (WME02), down 6 marks from 59 to 53 out of 75.

  • Mathematics: highest A boundary 64/75 (85%) on M1 Mechanics 1 (WME01), lowest 48/75 (64%) on P2 Pure Mathematics 2 (WMA12); highest A* boundary 68/75 on P3 Pure Mathematics 3 (WMA13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WDM11 A boundary up 7 marks since January 2024 (50 → 57); lowest raw mark for full UMS 60/75 on WMA12.
  • Further Mathematics: highest A boundary 68/75 (91%) on F2 Further Pure Mathematics 2 (WFM02), lowest 55/75 (73%) on F3 Further Pure Mathematics 3 (WFM03); highest A* boundary 71/75 on M3 Mechanics 3 (WME03); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WFM03 A boundary up 7 marks since January 2024 (48 → 55); lowest raw mark for full UMS 67/75 on WFM03.
  • Physics: highest A boundary 69/90 (77%) on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14), lowest 50/80 (62%) on U2 Waves and Electricity (WPH12); highest A* boundary 76/90 on U4 Further Mechanics, Fields and Particles (WPH14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WPH11 A boundary up 8 marks since January 2024 (53 → 61); lowest raw mark for full UMS 62/80 on WPH12.
  • Chemistry: highest A boundary 36/50 (72%) on U6 Practical Skills in Chemistry II (WCH16), lowest 30/50 (60%) on U3 Practical Skills in Chemistry I (WCH13); highest A* boundary 39/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Chemistry II (WCH16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WCH16 A boundary up 5 marks since January 2024 (31 → 36); lowest raw mark for full UMS 38/50 on WCH13.
  • Biology: highest A boundary 38/50 (76%) on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16), lowest 53/90 (59%) on U4 Energy, Environment, Microbiology and Immunity (WBI14); highest A* boundary 42/50 on U6 Practical Skills in Biology II (WBI16); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBI14 A boundary up 6 marks since January 2024 (47 → 53); lowest raw mark for full UMS 61/90 on WBI14.
  • Economics: highest A boundary 59/80 (74%) on U1 Markets in Action (WEC11), lowest 50/80 (62%) on U4 Developments in the Global Economy (WEC14); highest A* boundary 58/80 on U3 Business Behaviour (WEC13); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WEC13 A boundary up 6 marks since January 2024 (47 → 53); lowest raw mark for full UMS 62/80 on WEC14.
  • Business: highest A boundary 54/80 (68%) on U1 Marketing and People (WBS11), lowest 42/80 (52%) on U3 Business Decisions and Strategy (WBS13); highest A* boundary 54/80 on U4 Global Business (WBS14); biggest move against each paper's previous sitting: WBS12 A boundary up 9 marks since January 2024 (45 → 54); lowest raw mark for full UMS 52/80 on WBS13.
